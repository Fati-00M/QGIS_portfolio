How I work

Method & standards

A map is only useful if someone else can reproduce it and defend it. Every product above follows the same pipeline.

  1. 01

    Source & validate

    Open, citable datasets only — global settlement layers, glacier inventories, OpenStreetMap, satellite archives. Provenance, vintage and licence recorded before a layer enters a project.

  2. 02

    Process & verify

    Raster and vector geoprocessing in QGIS and Google Earth Engine, with Python where a step needs to repeat. Geometry, projection and attribute checks before styling begins.

  3. 03

    Design to standard

    Declared CRS, graticule, north arrow, scale bar, legend and date on every sheet. One symbology system across a series so products read as a coherent family, in colour or greyscale.

  4. 04

    Publish & narrate

    Delivered as print sheets, web-encoded animations or hosted dashboards — each paired with plain-language framing so a non-technical reader gets the finding, not just the graphic.
